CONCEPT

The Road Ahead is a narrative-driven puzzle adventure about grief, independence, and finding the courage to live again. On a farm in 1950’s rural America, you play as a widow who has isolated herself from the world after her husband’s death. She gradually rediscovers her confidence and independence by repairing her husband's old Model T and leaving their farm, eventually finding the courage to step back into the world on her own.
